# Letters for the owner to send: icons and frescoes

The Admin chat, 2026-11-11, on the arbiter's words of 12:57, 13:13 and 13:22
UTC: one short letter for every row of `docs/1.9-build/ICON-PERMISSIONS.md`
that has an address, and one courtesy letter to each of the five monasteries
whose frescoes and mosaics stay in the app by his word of 13:21 ("let's use
all the photos", `docs/1.9-build/notes/pictures-heritage-law.md`).

He sends each himself. What every letter says, and only this:

- what Purify is, and that the saints' lives and their pictures are free for
  every reader (`docs/1.9-build/free-and-plus.md`: the saints are free);
- that Purify also offers an optional subscription for some reading tools,
  and that the lives of the saints and their icons are never part of it (the
  arbiter's sentence of 14:07 UTC: told whole at the start, and the paid
  tier kept clear of the pictures on the record, as the heritage note
  advises);
- exactly which picture, as the list describes it;
- shown only where a reader meets the saint, in the app and on the website,
  with the credit they ask for, and never in advertising, a store listing or
  anything sold (his word with call 35);
- taken down at once if they ever ask;
- no fee is offered. If one is asked, it comes to him.

Replies go to legal@purifyapp.net. When a permission arrives in writing, the
icon goes on with a row in `lib/saints/iconRights.ts` (status "permission",
the letter as its evidence) and the saint leaves the list in
`lib/saints/__tests__/everySaintAPicture.test.ts` in the same commit.

Where a row asks for Greek or Romanian, the letter is given in that language
with the English beside it, which is the text he signs off. The Greek and
Romanian are the Admin chat's: if someone who writes either can read them
over before they go, better.

No letter yet, and why:

- **St Gabriel of Georgia:** no address read from the Georgian Patriarchate
  itself.
- **St Lazarus of Mount Galesion:** the only icon is imoph.org's, and no
  address for it was found.
- **Sts Zacchaeus and Alphaeus:** no icon found.
