The Fast

One screen, top to foot. The calendar's words for each day and the pastoral line are unchanged, and the page adds none of its own.

Why it looks this way
The choiceWhy
It opens as a chapter opens: the mark, a small line, the title, the short ruleIt is how the Bible and the Fathers' works open in the app. The Fast reads as Purify before a word is read.
Today leads, on the nightToday's fast is what a reader comes for. It stands first and alone, on the Nativity card's own ground.
The calendar's own mark beside the day's nameThe grapes, the fish and the wheat are the calendar's. A grey dot said nothing.
One switch in place of three pillsKept, In part and Broke are one choice, so they are one control: the carved switch Settings uses.
No mark wears a colourKept was green and Broke was red. A companion does not grade. All three are lit alike.
The count is quiet lines, not tilesA new reader reads one sentence under today. The second line appears with the first day kept.

The pastoral line in italics, under the switchIts words are unchanged. It stands where the mark is made, on its own type, with no box.
The way to the calendar is a line of its ownIt was a word inside a sentence. Now a thumb can find it.
