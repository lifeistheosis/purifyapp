# Saints waiting on a permission: the list for Admin's letters

Updates 2, 2026-11-11 (build 93 note 12; the owner's word of 2026-11-10,
"modern saints on the plate until a painter or monastery gives written
permission"). Each saint below has no picture in the public domain, so he
keeps the plate until a permission arrives in writing. For each: the best
modern icon found, who made it, where they can be written to, and what
matters for the letter. Every fact has the page it came from; a gap says
"not found", never a guess.

Admin writes one short letter per painter or monastery, for the owner to
send himself, asking written permission to show that one icon in the app
and on the website, with their credit. A fee, if asked, comes to the owner.
When a permission arrives, the icon goes on with a row in
`lib/saints/iconRights.ts` (status "permission", the letter as evidence),
and the saint leaves the list in
`lib/saints/__tests__/everySaintAPicture.test.ts` in the same commit.

The pre-build gate lets a saint through without a picture only if he is
here (CLAUDE.md, "No test build reaches him until it passes the pre-build
gate", part 3).

## St Paisios the Athonite (1924 to 1994)

- **The icon:** the Holy Hesychasterion of St John the Theologian at
  Souroti's own icon of him: half-length, in his black monastic cap and
  habit, his right hand at his breast, holding an open scroll with his
  saying that God works on the frequency of love and humility. A second
  Souroti icon on the same page shows him in the monastic hood.
  https://agiospaisios.gr/icons
- **Made by:** not named. The captions say only "of the Holy Hesychasterion
  of St John the Theologian in Souroti"; the icons carry a seal and a date
  in Greek numerals, no name.
- **To write to:** the contact form at https://agiospaisios.gr/contact, or
  by post: Ιερό Ησυχαστήριο Ευαγγ. Ιωάννης ο Θεολόγος, Βασιλικά, 57006
  Θεσσαλονίκη, Greece. Telephone +30 23960 41320 or 41315 (Tuesday,
  Thursday and Saturday, 10:00 to 12:00 and 12:30 to 14:00). No email is
  published. Wikipedia names agiospaisios.gr as the monastery's own site.
- **For the letter:** the monastery has a publishing house; the site sells
  no prints and states no licence. Write in Greek if possible.

## St Porphyrios of Kavsokalyvia (1906 to 1991)

- **The icon:** two icons by the theologian and iconographer Efstathios
  (Stathis) Giannis, published with his permission by the Church of
  Greece's Synodal Committee on Divine Worship for the canonization
  (January 2014): one standing between Kavsokalyvia and the hill of
  Kallisia, one half-length in the monastic hood with a staff and a
  pectoral cross.
  https://ecclesiagreece.gr/greek/holysynod/commitees/worship/stavrop_porfyrios.pdf
- **Made by:** Efstathios Giannis (the PDF thanks him for allowing
  publication). Which icon his convent at Milesi uses: not found.
- **To write to:** not found. The convent of the Transfiguration at Milesi
  is linked to porphyrios.gr, which does not answer (its certificate is
  wrong and it gives 404); porphyrios.net now leads elsewhere. The Synodal
  Committee on Divine Worship (ecclesiagreece.gr) published the icons with
  his permission and is the way to him.
- **For the letter:** ask the Committee for the painter's address, or for
  their permission if they hold it.

## St Silouan the Athonite (1866 to 1938)

- **The icon:** the iconostasis icon in St Silouan's chapel at Tolleshunt
  Knights, egg tempera on panel, 1988: half-length in the monastic hood and
  mantle, holding an open scroll ("I pray Thee, O merciful Lord, for all the
  peoples of the earth, that they may come to know Thee by Thy Holy
  Spirit"), lettered "Saint Silouan the Athonite".
  https://essexmonastery.org/articles/english/seeking-perfection-in-the-world-of-art-the-artistic-path-of-father-sophrony/
- **Made by:** Archimandrite Sophrony (Sakharov), now St Sophrony. The
  monastery's caption: "egg tempera on panel, by Father Sophrony, Tolleshunt
  Knights, 1988. Image © The Stavropegic Monastery of Saint John the
  Baptist, Essex". St Sophrony died on 11 July 1993 and was canonized in
  2019; the monastery holds the copyright it names.
- **To write to:** the Patriarchal Stavropegic Monastery of St John the
  Baptist, The Old Rectory, Rectory Road, Tolleshunt Knights, by Maldon,
  Essex CM9 8EZ, England (the Ecumenical Patriarchate's page for it).
  Telephone +44 1621 816471; the contact page
  (https://essexmonastery.com/contact-us/) has a bookshop form and warns
  that other messages may not be answered, so write by post.
- **For the letter:** the monastery sells icons and cards in its own
  bookshops (not yet online) and runs a publishing house. One letter can
  ask for St Silouan and St Sophrony together (St Sophrony is on the plate
  too: the November and December chat's page).

## St Cleopas of Sihăstria (1912 to 1998)

- **The icon:** the Romanian Patriarchate's official icon from the

- **To write to:** the Chancellery of the Holy Synod, Calea Șerban Vodă 31,
  040201 București; cancelaria@patriarhia.ro; +40 21 336 98 33
  (https://patriarhia.ro/contact/). For Sihăstria's own icon:
  sihastria@mmb.ro, 0233 251896 (https://sihastria.mmb.ro/contact). The

  (Colportaj.ro), and basilica.ro's terms forbid reuse without an
  agreement, so the Chancellery is the one to ask. One letter can ask for

## St Onesiphorus and Porphyrius

- **The picture:** the mosaic of the two in the Rotunda of St George at
  Thessaloniki, 5th century, both standing with their hands raised in prayer
  before a palace front. It is their own picture and shows no death. The
  only free file is 400 pixels wide
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ThessHagGeorgOnesiphPorphMos.jpg):
  cut to the two, the saints are small and soft beside a wide loss in the
  mosaic (docs/1.9-build/notes/build-93/pictures-onesiphorus-candidate.jpg,
  enlarged three times to be judged), and the arbiter's word of 2026-11-11
  is that it is not used. What is asked for is a full-size photograph of
  the same mosaic.
- **Made by:** the mosaic is of the 5th century, its makers unknown.
- **To write to:** the Ephorate of Antiquities of Thessaloniki City
  (Εφορεία Αρχαιοτήτων Πόλης Θεσσαλονίκης), the service of the Greek Ministry
  of Culture that keeps the Rotunda (the arbiter, 2026-11-11). Its office
  address and email: not found on a page that would open (the Ministry's
  page for the service refused the request, and its page for the monument
  did not answer). The Rotunda's own telephones, as the city's map of its
  UNESCO monuments gives them, are +30 2310 204868 and 2310 209545
  (https://thessaloniki.gr/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Unesco-Monuments-Map-GR-EN.pdf),
  read from a search result and to be confirmed before use.
- **For the letter:** in Greek. Ask for a photograph of the mosaic of Sts
  Onesiphorus and Porphyrius and leave to show it; Greek law has its own
  rules for pictures of monuments (docs/1.9-build/notes/pictures-heritage-law.md).
- **A modern icon, second:** two half-length figures holding crosses,
  captioned "Οἱ Ἅγιοι Μάρτυρες Πορφύριος καὶ Ὀνησιφόρος"
  (https://www.imoph.org/pdfs/IconologicalSynaxarion/11-November/09nov.pdf),
  unsigned, and no address for that Metropolis was found. oca.org's entry
  for the two has no icon.
- **Refused:** the fresco of their martyrdom at Dionysiou Monastery (1547,
  on Commons) is the martyrdom itself.
