### Full description

> Purify is an Orthodox Christian prayer book, Bible reader, and study library,
> built to open like a prayer book and not like a website.
>
> The whole core is free, with no ads and no tracking, and no account is required.
>
> THE DAILY LIFE
> • Today: the day's saint, fast, and appointed readings at a glance
> • Morning and evening prayers, the hours, and the akathists
> • The Nicene Creed and the full prayer book, searchable
> • The prayer rope, with a quiet haptic tick for every knot
> • The Church calendar: every feast, every fast, and the count to Pascha
>
> THE SCRIPTURES
> • The Bible with the Greek interlinear beside the English
> • Patristic commentary on the verse you are reading, from St. John Chrysostom and the Fathers
> • Bible search, including multi-passage lookup
>
> THE LIBRARY
> • The lives of more than fifty saints, with their writings to read in full
> • The Ecumenical Councils and the councils before Nicaea, in their own words
> • Theology studies, doctrinal topics, heresies answered, and apologetics
> • Reading positions remembered, so long works pick up where you left off
>
> PURIFY PLUS (optional)
> • An optional subscription that keeps the work going: the servers, the
>   production, the rights
> • Cross-device sync, custom florilegia and collections, and the enhanced
>   reading layer
> • The whole Orthodox core above stays free, always. Plus never gates
>   Scripture, prayers, saints, the councils, or the calendar
> • $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year. Cancel any time in Google Play
>
> YOUR READING, KEPT SAFE
> • Highlights, notes, and bookmarks are saved privately on your device
> • Sign in only if you want your reading synchronized across devices
> • Delete your account, and everything on our servers with it, any time
>
> Every text names its source. The translations are public-domain editions of the
> Fathers and the liturgical books; nothing is paraphrased or invented.

## Subscription disclosure (Play requires this near the buy point — in the paywall)

> Purify Plus is an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99/month or $29.99/year, charged
> to your Google Play account. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24
> hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in Google Play. The core
> of Purify stays free whether or not you subscribe.

## Data safety form

- **In-app purchases**: yes — an auto-renewing subscription (Purify Plus).
- Data collected: **Email address** (optional; account creation / app
  functionality; user can delete in-app).
- Data collected: **Purchase history** (subscription status, to grant entitlement).
- Data collected: **App interactions** (analytics, not linked to identity, anonymized).
- Data shared with third parties: **none** (RevenueCat/Google process the purchase
  as service providers, not for their own use).
- Data encrypted in transit: **yes** (HTTPS everywhere).
- Deletion path: in-app account deletion + local data clear.
