## What changed, and why it is the least

- **The reading room** waits for its book before it is shown, never in it. The stage's backdrop (the poster's own file) no longer carries an onLoad prop; it listens for its load on the element. An image with no onLoad is one React waits for while it switches pages (react-dom's suspensey images: it holds the page the reader is on, live, up to 800ms from the tap, until the picture has loaded and decoded). Once shown the stage waits for nothing but the day: a picture slower than React's wait fades in over its poster's plate, as every cover's does (the cover's own state after HOLD_MS until now). The owner's note 13 of build 80 ("Opening the reading room while its pictures load breaks the immersion") is kept by the wait before the room; the hole after it is gone.
- **Pray with chant** reads the library's index as the prayer opens (one file in the apps' bundle, read once a session) and its sheet rises once the index is in, or the read came back with nothing, so a tap is always answered.
