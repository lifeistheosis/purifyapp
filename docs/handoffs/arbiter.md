## Gotchas learned this shift

- An apply or fault chat reaches 200,000 tokens in 30 to 60 minutes and costs about $20 to $37. Tell each to send wide searches to a subagent.
- A message sent with priority "later" to a chat in a long turn can go unread until it rotates: Updates 30 never took two faults sent that way. Use "next", and carry anything routed into the successor's brief.
- A message to an archived session unarchives it: never write to an archived chat.
- A baton can be written before your last call reached the chat: check the successor's brief carries your calls (Updates 24's did not; Updates 25's brief did).
- `date -u` before every time you write: this shift wrote three times by memory and mended each.
- get_session's token count lags or overshoots; Fable chats estimate their own and run past the line during renders, which the rules allow (never rotate in the middle of a check or a render).
