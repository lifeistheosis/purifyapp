## Who controls the rights

Both the **New King James Version** (NKJV) and the **Orthodox Study Bible** (OSB) are owned and published by **Thomas Nelson**, an imprint of **HarperCollins Christian Publishing**. The OSB text is the NKJV; the study notes were commissioned by St. Athanasius Academy of Orthodox Theology and are co-licensed.

For app/website use you generally need two licenses:

1. **NKJV text license** from HarperCollins Christian Publishing (Bible Rights & Licensing).
2. **OSB study notes license** from Thomas Nelson, often handled together with the text.

## What to ask for

A digital app license that allows:

- Full NKJV text in the Old and New Testaments (Orthodox canon ordering with deuterocanon if available).
- OSB introductions, study notes, articles, glossary, maps, and lectionary in-app.
- Web and mobile distribution (iOS, Android, web). Specify both.
- Free-tier and paid-tier user counts (their licensing is usually tiered by users or revenue share).
- Offline reading (cached on device).

Be specific that this is a **commercial app** with a free tier and a paid tier. Pricing structures vary - typical app-Bible deals are revenue share (5-15%) or annual minimums in the low-to-mid four figures for small launches.
