THE READER MAP

What a reader is given because of who they are, written from the rules themselves
(lib/onboarding: flowSteps, readerType, road, week, plusIntro, space). Never edited by hand:
  READER_MAP_WRITE=1 npx vitest run lib/onboarding/__tests__/readerMap.test.ts
The plan it belongs to is docs/plans/reader-map.md.

1. THE QUESTIONS (a new reader, from the welcome, signed out)
  inquirer: welcome > account > background > level > intent > handoff
  learning: welcome > account > background > level > intent > handoff
  practicing: welcome > account > background > level > rule > intent > handoff
  no level: welcome > account > background > level > intent > handoff
  where the account's test asks it last: welcome > account > background > level > intent > handoff
  a priest in the United States, signed in: welcome > background > level > rule > role > clergyWhere > clergyJurisdiction > clergyParish > intent > reminders > handoff

2. THE READER'S TYPE (how far along they are; it decides how Today looks). The rule; not every pair is offered on the screens, see 3.
  inquirer: new, whatever the background
  learning: learning, whatever the background
  practicing: orthodox, whatever the background
  no level: orthodox (orthodox, prefer-not, not asked); new (catholic, protestant, christian, other-faith, none)

3. THE ROAD (from the first two answers; it decides the first week)
  The second question is asked in the reader's own terms, so each background is offered its own answers.
  orthodox, practicing: practicing
  orthodox, coming back: returning
  orthodox, preparing: catechumen
  orthodox, skipped: no road
  catholic, not yet been to a church: catholic
  catholic, going to a church: catechumen
  catholic, skipped: catholic
  protestant, not yet been to a church: protestant
  protestant, going to a church: catechumen
  protestant, skipped: protestant
  christian, not yet been to a church: christian
  christian, going to a church: catechumen
  christian, skipped: christian
  other-faith, not yet been to a church: newcomer
  other-faith, going to a church: catechumen
  other-faith, skipped: newcomer
  none, not yet been to a church: newcomer or seeker
  none, going to a church: catechumen
  none, skipped: newcomer or seeker
  prefer-not, completely new: newcomer or seeker
  prefer-not, learning: catechumen
  prefer-not, practicing: practicing
  prefer-not, skipped: no road
  not asked, completely new: newcomer or seeker
  not asked, learning: catechumen
  not asked, practicing: practicing
  not asked, skipped: no road

4. THE FIRST WEEK (seven pages, one a day; the last is the road's door)
  practicing: Your rule > Today's readings > The Psalter > The Church's year > Pray with the Fathers > Before sleep > Start with the Fathers
  returning: A simple morning rule > Today's readings > The Jesus Prayer > The Church's year > Before sleep > The Nicene Creed > Find a parish
  catechumen: The Trisagion Prayers > The Sign of the Cross > Your first Liturgy > The Nicene Creed > Today's readings > A simple morning rule > Find a parish
  catholic: Start with the Fathers > First Council of Nicaea > St. Leo the Great > The Jesus Prayer > The Excommunications of 1054 > Your first Liturgy > Find a parish
  protestant (bible): The Gospel of John > St. Ignatius of Antioch > First Council of Nicaea > What is prayer? > Council of Carthage (419) > The Council of Ephesus > Your first Liturgy
  protestant (liturgical): The Gospel of John > First Council of Nicaea > St. Leo the Great > The Jesus Prayer > Council of Carthage (419) > Your first Liturgy > Find a parish
  christian: The Nicene Creed > First Council of Nicaea > The Gospel of John > The Council of Chalcedon > The Estrangement of the Non-Chalcedonian Churches > The Trisagion Prayers > Your first Liturgy
  seeker: St. Ignatius of Antioch > Pentecost: the Descent of the Holy Spirit > First Council of Nicaea > The Council of Ephesus > Start with the Fathers > The Trisagion Prayers > Your first Liturgy
  newcomer: What is prayer? > St. Mary of Egypt > The Gospel of John > The Nicene Creed > The Sign of the Cross > The Church's year > Your first Liturgy

  A reader's own picks then arrange it (week.ts, weekFor). What each pick brings:
  saints: St. Mary of Egypt (practicing, returning, catechumen, newcomer); St. Leo the Great (catholic, protestant (liturgical)); St. Ignatius of Antioch (protestant (bible), christian, seeker)
  fathers: Start with the Fathers
  gospels: The Gospel of John
  councils: First Council of Nicaea
  history: Pentecost: the Descent of the Holy Spirit
  beliefs: The Nicene Creed
  liturgy: Your first Liturgy (inquirer, no level); The Trisagion Prayers (learning); The Church's year (practicing)
  feasts: Fasts and feasts
  prayer: A simple morning rule (inquirer, learning, no level); Morning prayers (practicing)
  quiet: The Jesus Prayer
  study: The Gospel of John (inquirer, learning, no level); The Book of Job (practicing)
