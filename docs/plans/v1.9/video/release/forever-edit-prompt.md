### How it should look and move

- **Light.** One key light from above and deep shadow. The phone comes out of darkness; its screen's glow falls on the ground under it. The light swells a little with each voice swell and eases back inside the bar. Soft pools only.
- **Camera.** Slow and continuous inside every bar: a push, a pull or an orbit, never still and never fast. A shallow depth of field so the ground is soft. Change the scale hard at each cut: close, then wide, then close.
- **Cuts.** Only on the measured hits. One cut at the top of each bar; at most one more inside a bar. The film's rhythm is the music's.
- **Pieces.** Every piece of a screen lands on a beat (0.65 s apart) and settles on the house curve before the next beat.
- **Type.** The app's sans, large, few words, one title in each bar, arriving late in the bar and gone before the next swell. Never a caption sitting over the phone the whole time.
- **Ground.** Night, with depth: the hill of the store pictures far off and out of focus, drifting slowly against the camera.
