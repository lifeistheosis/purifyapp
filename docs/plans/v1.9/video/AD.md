## The rules that bind it (the first cut's and the sample's, unchanged)

- **Real screens only.** The native export of main 6700c2e65, which is what the stores serve; a new reader with no Plus; Dark; Sunday 6 December 2026. Nothing of the app's interface is drawn.
- **Words** only from the drop, the store pictures' lines or the app's labels. No Plus feature is shown in use. The heritage list is read all through every take.
- **The moving phone.** It travels and settles on `--ease-house`. Nothing bounces, overshoots or spins, and a turn is no more than the store pictures' side stand. It is justified to him with its first test, since it is the one new thing to judge.
- **No** parchment, rays, side stripe, sparkle, pills or em dash. Phone first: 1080 by 1920, 60 frames a second, H.264, no sound stream.
- **One version.** Stills (the sheet) before the render.
- **Posting waits for two things:** prayer campaigns open to every reader (OWNER-STEPS R5c), since scene 8 shows them; and 1.9 live on both stores.

## Stages and price (estimates)

The five second sample, one screen with the machinery it had to build, cost about 259,000 tokens by get_session (the log, 2026-11-13). The machinery now exists, so a screen should cost less than that, and nothing here is measured yet.

- **Stage 1:** `npm ci`, the export of main, scenes 1 and 2 with the moving phone between them, a test of about 7 seconds and its stills, for his yes or no. About 150,000 to 250,000 tokens and 1 to 2 hours.
- **Stage 2, at his yes:** the other six scenes, the sheet, the render, and the page with Save the video. About 300,000 to 500,000 tokens and 2 to 3 hours.

**Where it is made** is his choice: one fresh chat for this one job with a cap for each stage (the arbiter's recommendation, since a fresh chat does not carry the arbiter's long history into every step), or here in the one chat (dearer for each step).
