# Purify 1.9: the social cards

One set for the drop, made on 2026-11-13 by `cards.mjs` in this folder, in the look he kept for the store pictures (`../README.md`, "The design"). His word, by the arbiter, 2026-11-13: one set, a cover, one card for each headline point, and a closing card with the store names. One version, no variants.

- **The stills** are in `stills/`: 1080 by 1920, 24-bit PNG, no alpha, for Instagram and TikTok. The small copies the admin panel's Drop tab shows are in `docs/plans/v1.9/cards/`, written by `node scripts/drop.mjs cards docs/plans/v1.9/store/cards/stills`.
- **The words** are all read from `docs/plans/v1.9/drop.json` when a card is made; none is written in `cards.mjs`. The headline points are the four the caption for the cards names.
- **The screens** are the 1.9 app itself, from the native export at `6633b789`, cut as the store build with Plus locked, as a new reader meets it on 6 December 2026. No interface is drawn. The card for Purify Plus shows the study sheet as a reader with no Plus sees it: each Plus part under its mark.
- **Held to:** checkMock on every card, after its self-test (contrast on five palettes, palette tokens, rays, side stripes, sparkle, em dashes, mirroring and the rest); no picture on the heritage list in view; no word the drop may never say, on a card or on a screen; the words clear of the screen and of the head and foot of a story, where an app's own buttons lie.
