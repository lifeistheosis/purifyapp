Before this is real

What it needsWhy
The road as one word on the countsToday no count says which road a reader is on. That was your choice of 11 November, and the counts say nothing of a reader's faith.
The privacy page, and a yes from readers in the EUIt is a count about faith. The page has to say it before anything is sent, and a reader in the EU has to agree first.
More readers than a road hasA count under twenty is not shown. Small roads go dark, as they do on this page. A road's numbers are to watch. A verdict comes from a test on all readers together.
Its place in AdminThis page shows the idea. In Admin it is drawn from the admin's own pieces, after your yes.

Why it is drawn this way

The choiceWhy, and what it does for you
A thread as thick as its readersThe eye finds where readers go, and where the web thins, before a number is read. Widths are to scale: a thread with half the readers is half as thick.
The count on the plate, the share beside the threadA plate is a place, and a thread is the way from A to B. So the figure that says how many went on stands on the way itself.
"Too few to show" under twentyIt is Admin's own rule today. A handful of readers is close to a description of people, and it is no finding either. On this page it also shows the limit: a small road goes dark by the middle of its week.
Every number marked as an exampleNothing is counted by road today. A drawing must not be taken for a result.
The same web as beforeOne picture to learn. The numbers are added to it, and nothing is moved.
