# Three edits studied, 2026-11-25

His word: "Study this video", then "not just the scene. but the editing, transition, emotion, quality, and etc (you don't have to study sound)", then two more ("then study this one", "then this one when you are done (new editing style)"). Read frame by frame with `docs/handoffs/promo-edits/analyze.py`, crops and strips; the films and stills are other people's work and stay out of git. His rules since: edits need not be public domain, and an edit carries its audio (CLAUDE.md).

## 1. The storm: Christ walks on the water (a TikTok download, 15.2 s)

Black and white, made by a video model, letterboxed: a 1080 by 740 picture in a 9:16 frame, so 61% of a phone screen is black. No words, no logo, no end card.

**The edit.** Eight shots, but no cut is ever seen:
- Cut inside a lightning flash (1.79 to 1.86, 4.86 to 4.88): the flash whites out the frame and the cut disappears into it. Every bright frame is the storm itself, never an added effect.
- Cut in darkness (about 6.8, and 11.36 to 11.38): near black on both sides, felt, not seen.
- Lightning as the reveal: through a 4.7 s dark shot He is seen only when lightning strikes (8.1, 9.1, 10.4), and the dark takes Him back.
- Each shot is closer than the last: a boat on a vast sea, a figure on the horizon, His bare feet on the water (the miracle shown as a detail), Him standing in the lightning, walking toward us in the dark, then His hand reaching out to the camera. The viewer is Peter.
- The camera is nearly still; a slow push in the last shot. No shake, no zoom transitions, no speed ramps. It ends on the picture.

**The emotion, built from light alone.** Grey sea (smallness), then darker and darker: the band's brightness falls to 5 to 9 of 255 from 6.7 to 11.4 s (losing Him in the dark: doubt). Then the hand, and a soft column of light rises behind Him for the last 1.3 s, still brightening on the last frame (rescue). Darkest just before the hand.

**Quality.** Strong: restraint, a whole Gospel scene with no word, every effect motivated by the story, ending in light. Weak: the feet do not touch the water (no splash, glossy skin), soft detail under TikTok's compression; 4.7 s of near black is a black screen on a phone in daylight; the letterbox wastes most of the screen. Christ is a photoreal Western face, kept mostly to His back, His silhouette, His feet and His hand. The last light is a soft pool, not rays: it passes the house rule.

## 2. The whisper: old-master paintings (a CapCut export, 16.4 s)

4:3 landscape (1440 by 1080), warm chiaroscuro, deep reds. Four Western oil paintings. It ends with CapCut's own logo card (1.9 s).

**The edit.**
- Each painting is cut like film coverage of a scene: a close-up of a face, an insert of hands, a two-shot, then the whole painting as the wide master. The second painting (5.3 to 9.7 s) takes four cuts, each wider than the last: a reveal by widening.
- Nothing is held still: slow eased pushes and pans on every picture. It opens mid-motion, a fast blurred move easing into a slow drift in 0.3 s.
- Between paintings: crush and develop. The picture's blacks are pulled down while its lights stay (5.2, 9.7, 11.9 s), a hard cut, and the next painting develops up out of the dark over about a second. Not a fade.
- The end: two angel paintings under a strobe of black frames (15 changes in 1.2 s, 13.2 to 14.4 s), black, the CapCut card.

**The emotion.** Intrigue. Its peak is a woman in white looking straight into the lens while another whispers in her ear (5.3 s): eye contact with the viewer. Then her hands, then the whole room.

**Quality.** Strong: still pictures filmed as if they were a scene; the widening reveal; clean crush-and-develop. Weak: 4:3 on a phone; the pushes go past the pictures' resolution (the red robe at 9.8 s is soft and blocky); the strobe is a beat effect of the kind he calls vibe coded, and fast black flicker is unkind to light-sensitive viewers; the CapCut card reads as a template.

## 3. Discipline: Musashi from Vagabond ("new editing style", a CapCut export, 18.6 s)

Square, 1080 by 1080. Coloured fan paintings and black-and-white manga panels. It ends with the maker's handle typed on black with a glow.

**The edit, his "new style".**
- Collage of mixed media: a coloured painting holds the body and the sky while black-and-white manga faces of the same man are swapped in place over it, about four a second (1.4 to 4.4 s). One face, many drawings.
- Two worlds: colour for the man's life (the sword, the forest), black ink on white for his inner voice. A white flash carries you from one to the other (4.6 and 11.0 s).
- Words: two short lines in comic lettering, set in the white space of the panel: "All truly strong people are kind." and "Do you see how infinite you are?" The last is a question to the viewer.
- It never stops moving (2% held frames). In the quote panels the frame moves in pulses, a push of a few pixels that eases out every 0.2 to 0.6 s, not a glide.
- Five kinds of transition in 18 s: white flash, a page-bend warp (7.8 s: the forest arrives bent like a sheet and flattens), a motion-blur whip (14.1 s), a fade to black, the typed handle.

**The emotion.** From crowded to calm: fast swaps, then a slow sword, a quiet dark forest, then white space and words, ending on a question and the signature. Stoic and inward.

**Quality.** Strong: the two worlds, words given room, the question at the end, a sign-off of its own. Weak: a sampler of stock effects (warp, whip, pulses), the vibe-coded look he has turned down; a glowing sign-off; square on a phone.

## What a Purify edit takes from the three

- From 1: tell it in light. Hide cuts in light or in dark, come closer each shot, end on the picture. No added effects.
- From 2: film one icon as a scene: the eyes, a hand, the inscription, then the whole icon. Crush and develop between pictures. The camera moves; the icon is never redrawn.
- From 3: two worlds, the saint's life in colour and the saint's own words in ink on a clean ground; a few real words, cited; a question to the viewer at the end; Purify's own sign-off, with no glow.
- Leave: the strobe, the warp, the whip, pulses on the beat, the glow, end cards from an editor, letterbox, 4:3 and square. Purify is 9:16 first.
- To ask him: a photoreal Christ made by a machine (as in 1) is his decision; the safer form keeps His face unseen and lets an icon carry it, for example a match cut from the reaching hand to the hand in the icon of Christ saving Peter.
- Cost: an edit like 1 needs generated footage (credits, priced before); 2 and 3 need pictures alone.
