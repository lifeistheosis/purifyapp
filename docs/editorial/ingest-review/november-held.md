# November's Lives held for a priest: ingest review

Generated by `node scripts/ingest-witness-lives.mjs november --held` from `scripts/menologion/november-held.mjs`. Do not hand-edit.

Each Life was typed from the page images of one scan of the printing, word by word against a second scan of the same printing, every difference settled on the page; the spec's header says which scans and what is left out. Below, the typed words are compared again with the scans that did not draft them: the second scan of the printing and the same text set again in other printings. A word they read differently and agree on would stop the run; a **misreading** is such a word that the page has as we have it. A **doubt** is a word no scan confirmed, most often the OCR's letters or another printing's spelling, listed here so a person can check any of them.
