Ruth is a short story set in the days of the judges. When famine drives Naomi's family to Moab and death leaves her a widow without sons, her Moabite daughter-in-law Ruth refuses to leave her, takes Naomi's people and Naomi's God as her own, and goes with her to Bethlehem. Gleaning in the harvest fields, Ruth meets Boaz, a kinsman of Naomi's husband, who takes up the duty of redeeming the family's land and marries her. Their son Obed was the grandfather of King David.

Ruth is one of the few women named in Saint Matthew's genealogy of Christ, which the Church reads on the Sunday before the Nativity. A woman of a foreign people brought into Israel by faith and love, she has long been seen as a figure of the nations gathered into the Church, and Boaz, the kinsman who redeems, as a figure of Christ the Redeemer. In this translation Naomi is Noemin and Boaz is Booz.
