Judges covers the unsettled years between the death of Joshua and the rise of the kings. Again and again the same circle turns: Israel forgets the Lord and serves other gods, falls under an oppressor, cries out, and is rescued by a leader the Lord raises up. Among these judges are the prophetess Deborah, Gideon, Jephthah and the mighty Samson. The book ends with dark stories of a people in disorder, under the refrain that Israel had no king in those days.

The Epistle to the Hebrews names Gideon, Barak, Samson and Jephthah among the heroes of faith. The Church's hymns see in Gideon's fleece, wet with dew while the ground around it stayed dry, a figure of the Theotokos, on whom the Word came down as silently as dew, and the story of the fleece is read at Vespers of Theophany.
