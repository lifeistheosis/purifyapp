Saint Paul wrote to the Ephesians from prison, most likely in Rome, to the church of the city where he had labored for some three years. The letter unfolds the mystery hidden for ages: God's plan to gather all things in heaven and on earth into one in Christ. The first half praises the Father who chose us in Christ before the world began, saved us by grace through faith, and broke down the wall between Jew and Gentile to make one new people, the Church, the body of Christ and the fullness of Him who fills all things. The second half draws out the life worthy of that calling: unity in one faith and one baptism, speech that builds up, marriage as an image of Christ and the Church, and the armor of God.

Its passage on marriage is the Epistle read at every Orthodox wedding. Saint John Chrysostom preached twenty-four homilies on the letter.
