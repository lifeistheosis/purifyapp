Joel, son of Bethuel, saw a plague of locusts strip the land bare and heard in it the warning of the great day of the Lord. He summons everyone, from the elders to the children at the breast, from the bride and bridegroom to the priests weeping between the porch and the altar, to a holy fast, and to a return to God with the whole heart and not with outward show, for the Lord is merciful and slow to anger. God answers with a promise: the land will be restored, and afterward He will pour out His Spirit on all flesh, so that young and old, sons and daughters, will prophesy and see visions.

On the day of Pentecost Saint Peter declared that this prophecy was being fulfilled before the eyes of the crowd, and the Church reads it at Vespers of Pentecost. Joel's call to the fast is also read in the week before Great Lent.
