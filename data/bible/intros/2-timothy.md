The Second Epistle to Timothy is Saint Paul's farewell, written from prison in Rome as he awaited his death. Nearly everyone has left him and only Luke is with him. He urges Timothy, his son in the faith, to stir up the gift of God given him through the laying on of hands, to share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ, to hold to the pattern of sound teaching, to handle the word of truth rightly, and to preach whether the time is favorable or not. He reminds him that all Scripture is breathed by God, and looks back on his own life as a contest fought to the end, a race finished and a faith kept, with a crown of righteousness waiting for him.

In the Divine Liturgy the Church prays for her bishops in words taken from this letter, that they may rightly teach the word of truth. Saint John Chrysostom preached ten homilies on it.
