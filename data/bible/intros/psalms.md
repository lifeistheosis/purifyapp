The Psalter is the prayer book of the Church. Composed largely by King David and other inspired authors, its 150 psalms - with the additional Psalm 151 in the Septuagint tradition - span lament, praise, repentance, thanksgiving, and prophecy of the Messiah.

In Orthodox practice the entire Psalter is divided into twenty kathismata and is read through every week of the liturgical year, and twice each week during Great Lent. The Church considers no service complete without psalmody: every Vespers, Matins, and Hour is woven from the psalms.

This edition follows Septuagint (LXX) numbering, which differs from the Hebrew numbering used in most modern English Bibles. From roughly Psalm 9 onward, an LXX psalm number is typically one less than the Hebrew equivalent (so the beloved "Lord is my shepherd" is Psalm 22 here, not 23). Cross-references for Psalms are omitted in this edition pending a numbering reconciliation.
