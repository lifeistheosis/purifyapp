Exodus (Greek: Ἔξοδος, "going out") tells of Israel's deliverance from slavery in Egypt under the prophet Moses, the giving of the Law at Mount Sinai, and the construction of the Tabernacle. It is the foundational story of God's covenant with His people.

The Church reads the crossing of the Red Sea as an icon of Holy Baptism, and the Passover lamb as a type of Christ - "Christ our Pascha is sacrificed for us." The Tabernacle, with its veil and ark, prefigures the Theotokos, who bore God within her.

Exodus is read at the Vesperal Liturgies of Holy Saturday and Pascha, when the Church relives the great passover from death to life.
