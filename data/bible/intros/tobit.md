Tobit is the story of a devout Israelite family in exile at Nineveh. Tobit gives alms and buries his murdered countrymen at the risk of his life, and is struck blind. Far away in Media, a young woman named Sara is mocked because a demon has killed seven husbands on her wedding nights. God hears the prayers of both and sends the archangel Raphael, in the guise of a kinsman, to travel with Tobit's son Tobias. Raphael guides him, frees Sara from the demon so that Tobias may marry her, and heals Tobit's eyes before revealing who he is.

The book is a school of everyday holiness: prayer joined to fasting and almsgiving, care for the dead, honor of parents, and marriage begun in prayer. Scripture names Raphael, one of the seven holy angels, only here, and the Church honors him with all the bodiless powers on November 8. Saint Polycarp echoed its teaching that almsgiving delivers from death.
