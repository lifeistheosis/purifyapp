The First Epistle to Timothy is a letter of Saint Paul to his beloved disciple, whom he had left in Ephesus to guard the church against false teaching. With the Second Epistle to Timothy and the Epistle to Titus it forms the Pastoral Epistles, the apostle's counsel to the shepherds of the Church. Paul asks that prayers be offered for all people and for those in authority, sets out what is required of bishops and deacons, gives directions for the care of widows and the honor of elders, and warns against the love of money. At its center stands a short hymn to the mystery of godliness, God revealed in the flesh and taken up in glory.

The Church's prayers at the Liturgy for all people and for those who govern answer Paul's charge here. The letter speaks to every bishop and priest, and to every Christian striving to keep the faith. Saint John Chrysostom preached eighteen homilies on it.
