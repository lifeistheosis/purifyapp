Fourth Kingdoms, called 2 Kings in most English Bibles, carries the history from the taking up of Elijah to the fall of Jerusalem. Elijah is taken into heaven in a whirlwind, parted from Elisha by a chariot of fire, and Elisha, with a double portion of his spirit, parts the Jordan, heals the spring of Jericho, raises the Shunammite woman's son, and cleanses the Syrian general Naaman in the Jordan. Then the northern kingdom falls to Assyria, the good kings Hezekiah and Josiah call Judah back to the Lord, and at last Babylon burns the Temple and carries Judah into exile.

The Church sees in these waters figures of Baptism. At Vespers of Theophany she reads of Elijah and Elisha crossing the Jordan, of Elisha healing the spring, and of Naaman, a stranger to Israel whom Christ Himself remembered, washed clean in the river. The raising of the Shunammite's son is read at the Vesperal Liturgy of Holy Saturday.
