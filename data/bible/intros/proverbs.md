Proverbs gathers the wisdom of Solomon and other inspired sages: short sayings, contrasts, and longer reflections on the fear of the Lord as the beginning of wisdom.

For the Fathers, the personified Wisdom of chapter 8 - "The Lord created me, the beginning of His ways, for His works" - is read as a foreshadowing of the Son of God, the eternal Wisdom by whom all things were made. This passage was at the heart of the fourth-century Arian controversy, and the Church's response, settled at Nicaea, insisted that Wisdom here is begotten, not made.

Proverbs is read at Vespers throughout Great Lent, alongside Genesis and Isaiah.
