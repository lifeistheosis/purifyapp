The shortest of the four Gospels, Mark moves with urgency from one event to the next - the word "immediately" appears more than forty times. Tradition holds that Mark wrote down the preaching of the apostle Peter, his spiritual father, and the Gospel reflects an eyewitness's vivid eye for detail.

Mark presents Christ as the Son of God revealed in deeds of power, who calls His disciples to take up the cross and follow Him to Jerusalem. Its opening verse - "The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God" - is itself a creed in miniature.

The Church reads Mark continuously during Great Lent and on weekdays in the post-Pentecost season. Mark is the patron evangelist of the Church of Alexandria.
